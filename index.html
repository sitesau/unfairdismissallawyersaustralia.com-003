A first enquiry you can copy

Use the contact route the page publishes, call, email, or quote form, and keep the first enquiry short enough that the reply will answer the same points across several listings. This template stays within the information the page itself uses.

  1. Category: I found your listing under [employment contract lawyers / unfair dismissal lawyers / workplace investigations].
  2. Issue: My concern is [short description in your own words].
  3. Documents: Please tell me what documents or notes you would want first.
  4. Next step: Please tell me whether the best next step is a call, an email exchange, or a formal appointment.
  5. Pricing: If your listing does not show enough pricing detail for comparison, please outline how you handle initial fees or quote requests.

This format helps when several Sydney listings appear plausible. Each reply should tell you whether the category fit was right, what material the provider wants first, and whether the published pricing detail needs further clarification.
